Edge AI computing on unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) faces a steep energy and thermal bottleneck. High-performance machine learning workloads—such as real-time computer vision, object classification, and sensor-fusion orchestration—are highly resource-intensive, pushing massive power demands onto edge hardware.

Translation-Ready Technology EmpowAI LLC resolves this physical power constraint with a proprietary, third-generation rotary engine designed specifically as a high-density, low-vibration hybrid range extender and auxiliary power unit (APU).
